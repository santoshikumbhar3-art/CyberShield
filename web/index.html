        How It Works

        CyberShield AI combines rule-based security analysis with contextual reasoning — never relying on a single point of failure.

        • You submit content

          Paste a suspicious message or URL into the Analyzer.

        • The security engine scans for indicators

          Known manipulation tactics (urgency, credential requests, impersonation) and URL structure red flags are checked against a transparent rule set.

        • Contextual analysis adds interpretation

          A reasoning layer explains which tactics were combined and why that pattern matters — and stays available even if not a single external service is reachable.

        • The risk engine combines every signal

          Each detected indicator contributes a fixed, visible weight to a 0–100 score. Nothing is randomized.

        • CyberShield generates an explanation

          You get both a plain-language summary and a technical breakdown of exactly what was detected.

        • You receive a recommended action

          Clear next steps — what to avoid, and what to do instead — matched to the risk level.

        About CyberShield AI

        The problem

        Phishing and scam messages are designed to exploit urgency and trust faster than most people can stop and think. Non-technical users are often left to decide, alone and under pressure, whether a message is safe.

        The solution

        CyberShield AI gives anyone a fast, explainable second opinion — a transparent risk score, plain-language reasoning, and a clear recommended action, all in seconds.

        Target users

        Anyone who receives messages or links they're unsure about — especially people without a cybersecurity background.

        Technology

        Built entirely on each language's standard library — no third-party frameworks, packages, or SDKs — to keep the project transparent, dependency-free, and easy to audit end to end.

        Responsible AI approach

        CyberShield AI never claims certainty it doesn't have. Every score is explainable, every URL verdict uses cautious language, and the system always remains useful even if any single component is unavailable.
